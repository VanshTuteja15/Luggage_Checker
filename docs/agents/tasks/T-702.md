## Goal (one paragraph, user-visible outcome)

Above the results, one short card answers the question in sentences: "Cheapest: $434.95 at Canada Luggage Depot (verified 9:02 a.m.). Amazon.ca shows $434.95 (web estimate)." It updates while prices stream in, and when the search was corrected it says so ("Showing results for “samsonite paralux medium”" with a button to search for what was typed). Every number in the card comes from the offers on the page (tested in `tests/engine/answer-v5.test.ts`); the card never invents or rewords anything.

## Contract (types/endpoints to use; do not change them)

- Props: `sentences: string[]` (render as given, in order, max 4; never build sentences in the component), `parsed: ParsedQuery` (`correctedFrom`, `didYouMean`), `onSearch?: (q: string) => void`, `running: boolean` (pass `view.status === "running" || view.status === "starting"`).
- The first sentence names the cheapest price; a sentence starting "The cheapest price is a web estimate" is a caution.

## Requirements (numbered, testable)

1. The first sentence is the card's headline (larger; emphasise the `$…` amount by wrapping it in `<strong>` with a regex over the given text: never re-format the number). Other sentences below in body size.
2. The caution sentence uses the estimate colour of `FreshnessBadge` (`text-warning`), with an icon that has `aria-hidden`.
3. While prices are still coming in, a muted "So far:" prefix on the headline; it goes when the search is done.
4. "Showing results for “…”" + "Search for “…” instead" (button, calls `onSearch(parsed.correctedFrom)`) when `parsed.correctedFrom` is set.
5. Nothing renders when there are no sentences and nothing was corrected (no empty card).
6. The card is a `<section aria-label="Answer">` and doesn't duplicate the results' `aria-live` announcements.
7. 375 px: no horizontal scroll; dark mode contrast AA.
